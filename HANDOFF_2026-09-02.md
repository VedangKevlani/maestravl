## Non-negotiables (unchanged, repeat to any new session)

- **Zero new paid services** beyond what's already free-tier-with-no-card,
  or explicitly flagged as paid-but-cheap and opt-in (MiniMax web search,
  ~$0.01/call).
- **Never fake success.** Nothing claims a provider confirmed, contacted,
  or resolved something it didn't actually verify. An `AgentRun` only
  reaches `CONFIRMED` when a human explicitly confirmed it.
- **Deterministic logic first, AI layered on later.** The deliberate
  exception is `lib/agents/replyInterpretation.ts` (Gemini) — reading what
  a reply *means* is genuine language understanding, not something a regex
  can do.

Note on where these non-negotiables live: earlier handoffs cite `AGENTS.md`
as "the original product spec." As of this session, `AGENTS.md` in the
